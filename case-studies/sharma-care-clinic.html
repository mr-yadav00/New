Sharma Care Multispeciality Clinic —
How We Built Kota's Most Trusted Medical Website

A zero-to-hero digital transformation for a multi-specialty clinic in Talwandi, Kota — complete with glassmorphism UI, local SEO dominance, and WhatsApp appointment booking.

View Live Demo ↗ Get a Similar Website

🔴 The Problem

When Dr. Arjun Sharma approached SiteLab India, Sharma Care Multispeciality Clinic had absolutely no online presence. In a city like Kota — where thousands of patients search Google daily for doctors, specialists, and clinics — being invisible online meant losing patients to competitors every single day.

The core challenges were stark and urgent:

  • Zero digital presence — the clinic didn't appear in any Google search results, not even for branded searches.
  • Competitors in Talwandi and greater Kota had websites, Google Business Profiles, and positive reviews — capturing all local search traffic.
  • Patients had no way to find clinic timings, specialties, or doctor information online before visiting.
  • No WhatsApp channel for appointment booking — all bookings happened only via phone or walk-ins, creating bottlenecks.
  • No social proof displayed — the clinic's excellent 4.9★ patient reviews were trapped in paper registers, invisible to potential new patients searching online.
  • Mobile users searching "best doctor near me" or "Internal Medicine doctor Kota" found nothing about the clinic.

The result? Every day without a website was a day of lost patients, lost revenue, and lost trust. The clinic needed a complete digital overhaul — not just a brochure page, but a patient-conversion machine.

✅ Our Solution

SiteLab India designed and delivered a complete, premium medical website for Sharma Care Multispeciality Clinic — built from scratch with the glassmorphism design language, optimized for local search dominance, and engineered for patient trust from the first click.

The website was built to accomplish three primary goals: look credible, rank on Google, and convert visitors into appointment bookings via WhatsApp.

Every design decision — from the glassmorphism card panels to the floating WhatsApp button — was made with Kota's local patients in mind. The result is a digital presence that communicates authority, warmth, and accessibility simultaneously.

Delivery was completed within 72 hours of project confirmation. The client received a fully hosted, SEO-ready medical website with zero monthly platform fees.

🔍 SEO Strategy

The SEO strategy for Sharma Care Clinic was built around capturing high-intent local searches from patients in Kota who are actively looking for medical services. We focused on three layers: on-page optimization, technical SEO, and local SEO.

Primary Keywords Targeted:

Internal Medicine Doctor Kota Best Doctor Talwandi Kota Multispeciality Clinic Kota General Physician Kota Doctor near me Talwandi Clinic appointment Kota
  • Title Tag Formula: "[Primary Keyword] | [Clinic Name] — [Location]" applied to every page for maximum click-through from SERPs.
  • Meta Descriptions: Custom-written 155-character meta descriptions for every page, including emotional triggers and local identifiers.
  • H1/H2 Structure: Semantic heading hierarchy optimized for both readability and crawler comprehension.
  • Schema Markup: MedicalBusiness, Physician, and LocalBusiness JSON-LD schemas implemented to power Google's knowledge panel.
  • Google My Business Optimization: GMB profile set up with all NAP details, categories, services, photos, and review response templates provided.
  • Internal Linking: Strategic internal links between services pages, doctor profiles, and the contact page to distribute link equity.
  • Image SEO: All images compressed, WebP-converted where possible, and tagged with descriptive alt text including local keywords.

🤖 GEO Strategy (AI Search Optimization)

Beyond traditional SEO, we implemented a Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) strategy to ensure Sharma Care Clinic appears in AI-powered search results from Google SGE, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and voice assistants — which are increasingly becoming how patients discover local doctors.

  • FAQ Schema for AI Search: 8 patient questions written in natural conversational language and marked up with FAQ schema — directly targeting AI-generated answer boxes.
  • Entity Optimization: Dr. Arjun Sharma established as a named medical entity with consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) signals across all pages.
  • Structured Answers: Key medical information written in a direct, answer-first format that AI models prefer when generating responses to queries like "who is the best doctor in Talwandi Kota?"
  • Knowledge Panel Signals: Schema markup, consistent citations, and entity linking configured to help Google generate an accurate knowledge panel for the clinic.
  • Voice Search Optimization: Content written in conversational phrases matching how patients ask questions on Siri, Google Assistant, and Alexa.

📱 Mobile Optimization

Over 80% of local searches for doctors and clinics happen on mobile devices. Sharma Care Clinic's website was built mobile-first — meaning every design decision started with the smallest screen and scaled up, never the reverse.

  • 100% mobile-responsive layout tested on Android and iOS across 12 screen sizes from 320px to 1440px.
  • Floating WhatsApp button visible at all times on mobile — positioned to not obscure content but remain instantly tappable.
  • Click-to-call emergency number prominently displayed in the hero section for patients with urgent needs.
  • Sticky bottom navigation bar on mobile with WhatsApp, Call, and Book Appointment quick-access buttons.
  • Font sizes auto-scaled using CSS clamp() for perfect readability on every device without horizontal scrolling.
  • Fast loading: images optimized, CSS minified, no render-blocking scripts — target load time under 2.5 seconds on 4G.
  • Touch-friendly tap targets (minimum 44×44px) for all buttons and interactive elements.

🛠️ Technology Stack

The website was built using a modern, lightweight stack chosen specifically for performance, SEO-friendliness, and zero ongoing hosting costs for the client:

HTML5 Semantic Markup CSS3 Glassmorphism Vanilla JavaScript JSON-LD Schema Google Fonts (Poppins) Google Maps Embed API WhatsApp API Integration GitHub Pages Hosting

The decision to use pure HTML/CSS/JS (rather than a CMS like WordPress) was deliberate: it results in faster load times, better Core Web Vitals scores, zero plugin vulnerabilities, and no recurring subscription fees — all critical factors for a local business website that needs to rank and perform reliably.
