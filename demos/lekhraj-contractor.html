Project Overview

About This Project

Lekhraj Contractor is a precast boundary wall and industrial construction specialist operating across Kota and Rajasthan. Prior to this website, the business had zero online visibility — all leads came through personal contacts and word of mouth, leaving significant market share on the table.

SiteLab India built a striking industrial-themed website with a bold gold-on-dark aesthetic inspired by the materials and craft of construction. The running marquee text banner, heavy typography (Barlow Condensed + Bebas Neue), and sectioned project gallery communicate strength, reliability, and scale.

Every section — from services showcase to manufacturing strengths to coverage area — is designed to answer the key questions that industrial clients and real estate developers ask before choosing a contractor.
