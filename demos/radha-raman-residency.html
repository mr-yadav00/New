Project Overview

About This Project

Radha Raman Residency is a student PG accommodation provider in Kota — a city famous across India for its coaching institutes preparing students for JEE, NEET, and other competitive exams. With thousands of students arriving in Kota every year, the PG accommodation market is highly competitive.

SiteLab India built a clean, trust-inspiring website designed specifically for the student audience. The light, welcoming design communicates safety and comfort — exactly what parents and students look for when choosing accommodation. A prominent room gallery, transparent pricing table, detailed amenities list, and student testimonials all work together to build immediate confidence.

The WhatsApp booking button, nearby coaching institutes mention, and LocalBusiness schema ensure the residency appears in Google searches for "PG near Resonance Kota", "student hostel Kota", and similar high-intent queries from incoming students.
