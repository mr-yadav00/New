Project Overview

About This Project

Yadav Fitness Club is a premium gym located in Jawahar Nagar, Kota. Before working with SiteLab India, the gym relied entirely on word-of-mouth referrals — with no online presence to showcase their world-class facilities, qualified trainers, or competitive membership plans.

SiteLab India built a dark, high-energy sports-aesthetic website using real gym photography. The hero section with the gym interior image immediately communicates energy and ambition. The membership pricing matrix makes plans instantly comparable, reducing friction to sign up.

Every section — trainer profiles, class schedule, facilities gallery, and testimonials — builds the confidence that pushes potential members to tap the WhatsApp button and inquire about membership.
