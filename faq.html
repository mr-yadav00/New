🔍 SEO & Local SEO

Local SEO is the process of optimizing your website to appear when people search for services near them — like "best doctor in Kota", "gym near Talwandi", or "web designer Rajasthan".

Local SEO includes:

  • Google Business Profile optimization (the map/listing that appears)
  • Location-specific keywords in your website content
  • LocalBusiness schema markup for search engines
  • NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone) across the web
  • Local citation building and directory listings

Without local SEO, your competitors in Kota will appear before you even when customers are nearby and ready to buy.

Yes! SiteLab India provides comprehensive SEO services for businesses in Kota and across Rajasthan. Our SEO services include:

  • Keyword research for your industry and location
  • On-page SEO (meta titles, descriptions, headings, schema)
  • Google Business Profile setup and optimization
  • Local citation building
  • Monthly SEO reports with rankings and traffic data
  • Content strategy and blog post creation

Visit our SEO Services Kota page for detailed information and pricing.

SEO results timelines vary by competition level and starting point:

  • Local SEO — 2–4 weeks for Google Business Profile improvements and local pack visibility
  • Competitive local keywords — 1–3 months to see page 1 movement
  • Regional/national keywords — 3–6 months for consistent, significant ranking improvement

SEO is a compounding investment — the results grow stronger over time. Businesses that start SEO now will dominate their local market in 6–12 months.

Yes. Google Business Profile (GBP) setup is available as an add-on for ₹799. This includes:

Schema markup (also called structured data) is special JSON-LD code added to your website that helps search engines understand your content more precisely — and present it better in search results.

Benefits of schema markup:

  • ⭐ Star ratings appearing in Google search results
  • ❓ FAQ accordions expanding directly in search results
  • 📍 Business address and hours in the knowledge panel
  • 🔗 Breadcrumbs showing your site structure in results
  • 🤖 Better understanding by AI systems for GEO optimization

Schema markup is included in our Standard and Premium packages. It's one of the most impactful SEO improvements you can make. Learn more on our blog.

🤖 GEO & AI Search Optimization

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization — the process of optimizing your website and digital presence to appear in AI-generated answer systems like:

  • 🤖 ChatGPT (OpenAI) — when users ask about businesses in your field
  • 🔮 Google Gemini — AI Overviews in Google search results
  • 🔍 Perplexity AI — the AI-native search engine
  • 🗣️ Microsoft Copilot — AI assistant with web browsing
  • 📱 Voice assistants and AI-powered apps

As more people ask AI instead of Googling, GEO becomes critical. A business properly optimized for AI answers will be recommended to thousands of users who never visit Google at all.

Learn more on our GEO Authority page.

Traditional SEO
  • 🎯 Target: Google blue links
  • 📊 Goal: Page 1 rankings
  • 🔑 Focus: Keywords & backlinks
  • 📈 Metric: Click-through rate
  • ⏱️ Timeline: 3–6 months
GEO Optimization
  • 🎯 Target: AI-generated answers
  • 📊 Goal: AI citations & mentions
  • 🔑 Focus: Entities & structured content
  • 📈 Metric: AI answer appearances
  • ⏱️ Timeline: 1–3 months

The ideal strategy combines both SEO and GEO for maximum digital visibility in 2026 and beyond.

Yes. SiteLab India is one of the very few web design agencies in Rajasthan implementing GEO optimization. Our Premium websites include:

  • FAQ Cluster Pages — Comprehensive question-answer content that AI systems love to cite
  • Entity Optimization — Building your business as a recognized entity in knowledge graphs
  • Structured Data — Multiple JSON-LD schema types for maximum AI comprehension
  • Authority Signals — Citations, consistent NAP, and authoritative content linking
  • Content Depth — Long-form, comprehensive content that establishes expertise

With proper GEO strategy implemented, yes — your business has a strong probability of being mentioned in AI-generated answers when users ask about your service category and location.

What affects AI citation probability:

  • ✅ Comprehensive FAQ content about your industry
  • ✅ Strong structured data and schema markup
  • ✅ Authoritative entity signals (consistent citations, GBP, reviews)
  • ✅ High-quality, original informational content
  • ✅ Mentions on credible websites and directories

While no one can guarantee specific AI placements (AI systems change frequently), SiteLab India's GEO strategy is designed using current best practices and active research into how AI systems select sources.

Entity SEO is the process of making your business a clearly defined, verifiable entity in search engine and AI knowledge graphs — similar to how Wikipedia entries establish facts about real-world things.

For your business, entity SEO means:

  • Google/AI understands exactly what your business is, what it does, and where it is
  • Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across all platforms, directories, and your website
  • Schema markup that explicitly declares your business type, location, and services
  • Mentions and citations across trusted Indian business directories
  • A Google Knowledge Panel (the info box on the right side of search results)

Entity SEO makes you findable by both traditional search engines and next-generation AI assistants.
