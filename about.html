Our Mission

Built for Real Impact, Not Just Pretty Pages

SiteLab India was born out of a frustration. Local doctors in Kota were paying ₹20,000–₹80,000 to big agencies for bloated WordPress sites that loaded slowly, ranked nowhere on Google, and had zero WhatsApp integration — the single most important lead channel for Indian businesses.

Our mission is to close that gap. We build lean, fast, and SEO-structured web systems that actually generate appointments, enquiries, and calls — not just digital brochures. Every pixel, every schema tag, every CTA is placed with a conversion purpose.

We serve doctors, dental clinics, fitness gyms, building contractors, PG hostels, and real estate agents — anyone in Kota, Rajasthan who wants to be found on Google and turn that traffic into real business.

How We Work

Our 5-Step Process

From your first WhatsApp message to a fully live, Google-indexed website — here's exactly how every SiteLab project unfolds.

01

Discovery & Goal Setting

We start with a free consultation via WhatsApp or call. We learn about your practice, target patients or customers, existing competition in Kota, and specific conversion goals — appointments, calls, enquiries, or form fills.

02

Design & Blueprint

Based on your brief, we design the full visual layout — section structure, color palette, typography, and content hierarchy. You receive a live preview link to review the blueprint before we write a single line of final code.

03

Development & SEO Build

Kapil personally hand-codes your entire website in clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Simultaneously, we add all SEO infrastructure: schema markup, meta tags, sitemap, robots.txt, and local keyword integration for Kota searches.

04

Review, Pay & Launch

You review the complete, fully functional website. Request any changes — we implement them at no extra cost. Only after you're 100% satisfied do you pay and approve the launch. We then publish to your domain and submit to Google Search Console.

05

Post-Launch Support

After your site goes live, we monitor Search Console for indexing, help you set up Google Business Profile, and remain available on WhatsApp for content updates, seasonal changes, and any future enhancements your growing business needs.
