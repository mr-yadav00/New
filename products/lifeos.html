Core Features

Everything You Need to Run Your Life

LifeOS bundles the most powerful productivity tools into one seamless experience — built for ambitious people who want real results, not just another to-do list.

📅

Goal Tracking

Set annual, monthly, and weekly goals with milestones and deadlines. Track progress visually with percentage completion bars. Know exactly where you stand on every goal at any given moment.

✅

Task Management

Daily task lists with priority levels — High, Medium, Low. Organize by project, tag, or deadline. Never forget what matters most. Build a clear, distraction-free daily work queue.

📊

Habit Builder

Build powerful daily routines with streak tracking and consistency scoring. Consistency creates transformation — LifeOS makes the compounding visible and keeps you accountable every single day.

🧠

AI Insights

Smart suggestions based on your progress patterns, habit streaks, and goal completion rates. Work smarter, not harder. LifeOS learns what works for you and surfaces actionable recommendations.

📈

Progress Analytics

Beautiful charts and dashboards showing your growth over time. Celebrate small wins and identify trends. Understand your productivity patterns and optimize your peak performance hours.

🔒

Privacy First

Your data stays on your account. No selling, no sharing, no third-party access — ever. LifeOS is built with privacy as a core architectural principle, not a marketing promise.
